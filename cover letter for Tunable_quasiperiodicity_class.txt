Dear Editors,

I am pleased to submit our manuscript titled “Arithmetic Tuning of Dynamical Critical Exponents in Quasiperiodic Localization Transitions” for consideration for publication in Physical Review A.

In this work, we investigate how the arithmetic structure of the irrational modulation affects the critical behavior of quasiperiodic localization transitions. We consider the Aubry-André-Harper model with a family of generalized Fibonacci irrational frequencies generated by the recursion (F_{k+1}=nF_k+mF_{k-1}). This construction allows us to tune the incommensurate frequency in a systematic way while keeping the microscopic Hamiltonian unchanged.

By combining generalized fidelity susceptibility, localization-length scaling, and finite-size gap analysis, we show that the correlation-length exponent remains robust and consistent with the standard Aubry-André-Harper value, (\nu\simeq 1), for different irrational modulations. In contrast, the dynamical critical exponent z, extracted independently from the fourth-order generalized fidelity susceptibility and from the low-energy gap scaling, varies systematically with the arithmetic parameters of the generalized Fibonacci sequence. These results reveal a separation between static and dynamical critical scaling: the localization fixed point remains unchanged, whereas the dynamical scaling can be tuned solely through the irrational arithmetic of the quasiperiodic modulation.

Our findings demonstrate that the incommensurate frequency is not merely a technical ingredient for generating quasiperiodicity, but can serve as a physically relevant control parameter for dynamical criticality. This provides a simple and experimentally relevant route to tuning critical dynamics in deterministic aperiodic systems without adding disorder, interactions, non-Hermiticity, pairing, or external fields. The predicted tunability of z may be probed in platforms such as ultracold atoms in optical lattices and photonic quasicrystals through wave-packet spreading and anomalous quantum transport.

Given the novelty of tuning dynamical critical behavior by irrational arithmetic alone, we believe that this manuscript will be of significant interest to the readership of Physical Review A. We kindly request that you consider it for publication in your journal.

Thank you for your time and consideration. We look forward to your response.
